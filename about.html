A community-built rental price map for Nagpur. Real rents, shared by real residents. No broker. No BS. 🍊

Our Mission

Make rent transparent in Nagpur.

Every renter deserves to know what the market actually looks like — not what a broker tells them. We're building the community database that makes that possible.

The Problem We're Solving

Rental prices in Indian cities are completely opaque. There is no public database, no registry, no way to verify what a fair price looks like in any given neighbourhood. Brokers control this information and use it to their advantage.

The result: tenants overpay. New residents get taken advantage of. Negotiation happens in the dark. And the information asymmetry keeps growing.

nagpur.rent flips this. Every pin on the map is a Nagpurkar saying — "this is what I actually pay." No middleman. No agenda. Just data.

What nagpur.rent Does

📌
Rent Pins
Residents drop anonymous pins showing their actual rent — gated, non-gated, BHK type, furnishing.
📊
Percentile Ranking
Every pin shows how that rent compares to others in the same BHK category across Nagpur.
🔍
Flat Hunt
Looking for a flat? Drop a seeker pin. Landlords and residents find you directly.
🚨
Naka Alert
Community-reported police checkpoints across Nagpur. Auto-expires in 7 days.
🏠
Property Listings
Verified commercial and residential listings — direct from owners, no broker fees.
✍️
Rent Blog
Area guides, rent trends, and practical tips based on real community data.
